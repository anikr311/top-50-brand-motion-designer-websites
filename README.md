## FAQ

**What is the best Brand & Motion Designer website?**
Sander van Dijk (sandervandijk.tv) ranks #1 — a solo brand animation specialist for Airbnb, MoMA, Google and IKEA.

**What makes a motion designer portfolio premium?**
Strong typography, motion on the site itself, editorial layout, moving case-study tiles, clear positioning, and one repeated call to action.

**Are all 50 websites live?**
49 homepages were verified live on 17 November 2026; Mubien (#20) is index-verified from its October 2026 launch posts.

## About
Selection criteria: the designer must combine brand identity AND motion/animation, the website must be live, modern and premium (award-level polish), and templated / Behance-only portfolios were excluded.
