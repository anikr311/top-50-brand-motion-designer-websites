How this list was built

The bar

  • Both disciplines, proven: each person or studio explicitly offers or shows brand identity and motion/animation on its own site.
  • Premium: award-level or near award-level polish — Awwwards / FWA / CSSDA recognition where available, strong typography, motion on the site itself, editorial layout.
  • Excluded: Behance/Dribbble-only profiles, Linktree, Adobe Portfolio and templated grids, dated WordPress portfolios, pure illustrators, pure UI/UX, pure 3D artists without branding, and agencies of 50+ people (two labelled benchmark exceptions).

Verification notes

  • Huncwot (#38) — fetch returned a May 2026 crawl; Nov 2026 directories still list the studio and site as active.

Notable exclusions

  • Jessica Walsh (personal) — no separate personal portfolio site exists; her vehicle is the agency &Walsh, included at #29 as a labelled benchmark.
  • Animade & Gunner — both acquired by Duolingo; no longer independent live portfolios.
  • Base Design, BOND — multi-office networks (~100–190 people) exceed the size cap.
  • Jake Bartlett, Dann Petty — their sites are now a course site and a product-design portfolio respectively, not brand+motion portfolios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Brand & Motion Designer website?

Sander van Dijk (sandervandijk.tv) ranks #1 — a solo brand animation specialist whose animated logo grid for Airbnb, MoMA, Google and IKEA makes “brands in motion” the whole identity of the site.

What makes a motion designer portfolio premium?

Strong typography, motion on the site itself, editorial layout, case studies shown as moving tiles or films, clear one-line positioning, and one repeated call to action.

Are all 50 websites live?

Yes — 49 homepages were verified live on 17 November 2026; Mubien (#20) is index-verified from its own October 2026 launch posts.
